A TASTE OF TROPISUEÑO

substitutions

Vegan & Vegetarian Options

We’re happy to make substitutions to better accommodate your group’s preferences.

Included at no additional cost

The following vegan substitutions are available at no additional cost:

  • Chicken Flautas → Jackfruit Flautas
  • Pork Chile Verde → Jackfruit Chile Verde

Vegan & vegetarian entrées | +$5 per person

For the entrée options, we offer the following vegan/vegetarian substitutions for an additional $5 per person:

  • Jackfruit Enchiladas
  • Tofu Potato Enchiladas
  • Veggie Mix Enchiladas
☆

Most popular upgrade

We recommend upgrading the Veggie Mix to the Tofu Potato Enchiladas, which is our most popular option.

✺

You’re also welcome to order additional vegetarian items à la carte for the table.

ORDER AS YOU GO

Beverages on Consumption

Beverages are charged based on consumption.

$15 minimum beverage spend per guest, calculated across the total number of guests in the group.

This minimum may be met with any beverages from the menu, including alcoholic and N/A options.
