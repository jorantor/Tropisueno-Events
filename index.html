Event FAQS

How many guests can Tropisueño accommodate?

Tropisueño can accommodate up to 250 guests for a cocktail-style Full Restaurant Buyout. Our indoor space can accommodate up to 85 seated guests, while our covered patio is ideal for up to 65 guests cocktail-style.

What types of private events do you host?

We host corporate receptions, conference events, company dinners, happy hours, holiday parties, networking events, product launches, brand activations, birthdays, wedding celebrations, and other social events.

Can we host a multi-day event or activation?

Yes. Tropisueño can accommodate multi-day events and brand activations, subject to availability. Our team can work with you to coordinate access, setup, breakdown, and event service across multiple days.

Is there a food and beverage minimum?

Yes. Minimums vary depending on the space, date, guest count, and demand. Higher minimums may apply during major San Francisco conferences and citywide events.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Yes. We can accommodate many vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and other dietary needs. Please share your guests' dietary restrictions with us before the event so we can plan accordingly.

Can we bring decorations and brand the venue?

Yes, you are encouraged to bring any company signage, decorations, displays, branded materials, photo moments, and other approved installations.

Can we bring our own AV, DJ, or entertainment?

Yes. You are welcome to work with approved third-party vendors for AV, DJs, entertainment, photography, and other event production needs.

Can you recommend event vendors?

Our team provides the venue, food and beverage, and service staff. Additional services are arranged directly by the client. We may be able to recommend vendors who have previously worked at Tropisueño.

Is parking available nearby?

Yes. Tropisueño is located on Yerba Buena Lane in downtown San Francisco, with multiple public parking garages available within walking distance of the restaurant.
